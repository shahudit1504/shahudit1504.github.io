GWSCLI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

GWSCLI is a personal-use command-line application built and used exclusively by its developer, Udit Shah, to access his own Google Workspace data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, Slides, Tasks) via the Google APIs.

Data access and use

  • GWSCLI accesses only the developer's own Google account data.
  • Data retrieved is used solely for personal productivity purposes (e.g. reading/organizing email, calendar events, and files).
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party or external service.
  • No data is used for advertising purposes.

Data storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the developer's own device, encrypted at rest.
